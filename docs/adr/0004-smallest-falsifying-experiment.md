# ADR 0004: Run the smallest experiment that can falsify the current repair

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Issue: [#19](https://github.com/jarredparrett/narrative-game-library/issues/19)

## Context

The Winter Observatory climb repeatedly regenerated and rejudged a nineteen-
artifact package when the observed defect belonged to one artifact or one
shared capability. That preserved caution but mixed diagnosis with standing,
spent model calls on unaffected bytes, and asked for human direction too often
or too late.

The library already preserves exact Candidates, frozen instruments, external
Verismill evidence, human approvals, and separated quality standings. It needs
an execution policy that uses that evidence without weakening it.

## Decision

Every active climb names exactly one qualification target. Each finding routes
to a default owner and loop. A broader loop is valid only with a persisted
reason.

Before execution, typed contract changes derive the minimum rebuild,
remeasurement, and replay set. Content-identical outputs carry prior evidence
forward. Player-critical evidence receives fresh blind gameplay; host-only
clarification does not. Instrument changes create a new, non-comparable
standing lineage.

Diagnosis uses a bounded preflight over one or two representative units.
Preflight has explicit iteration, model-call, token, improvement, escalation,
and park rules and cannot claim standing. Three repeated non-improving failures
of one structural class escalate to the owning capability issue by default.
The accepted baseline remains selected.

Formal measurement freezes one exact Candidate, excludes its fixers from the
judge panel, and runs the complete instrument once. Independent model calls may
run concurrently, but receipts are persisted and aggregated in deterministic
authority order.

Human approval remains first-order at four boundaries: target and instrument,
repair tranche, completed exact Candidate, and disposition. One tranche
approval permits autonomous bounded iteration inside that scope.

## Consequences

- A local artifact tell no longer implies a whole-world rebuild.
- Operators can see the active target, route, invalidation, remaining budget,
  stop reason, and next authorized transition in derived projections.
- Preflight evidence cannot be confused with formal standing.
- Broader evaluation remains available when a contract change requires it; the
  reason becomes auditable rather than ambient.
- The policy saves work only when hashes and dependency declarations justify
  carry-forward. Missing dependency knowledge must broaden scope explicitly.

## Worked falsification

The Winter Observatory shared handwriting-register finding routes first to
`night_observing_log` as a representative renderer benchmark. The bounded plan
permits at most three builds and six diagnostic calls, then one three-judge
formal panel after human review. The recorded history used 76 builds across
four nineteen-artifact preflights and 111 full artifact-judge calls across the
Candidate 3 and Candidate 6 formal paths. The comparison makes no quality
claim: a pass still requires the formal instrument, and unchanged results carry
forward only by exact content hash.
