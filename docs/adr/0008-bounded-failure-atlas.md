# Improve evaluation through a bounded Failure Atlas

Agent playtest traces may recursively propose Failure Signals, Attributions,
Classes, detectors, and Challenge Cases, but they do not automatically rewrite
games, rewards, rubrics, or standing. Only a versioned Atlas revision supported
by replay-verifiable evidence, counterexamples, independent corroboration, and a
sealed-suite check changes future experiments; historical measurements retain
their original Panel, rubric, and Atlas versions. This preserves compounding
evaluation knowledge without allowing the measuring system to teach itself how
to pass its current test.
