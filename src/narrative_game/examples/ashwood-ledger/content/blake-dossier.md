# Blake Mercer - Estate Counsel

You represent the trustees of the fictional Vale House Archive. The trustees planned to sign a redevelopment option this afternoon. A missing property ledger could conceal a title problem and make that decision irresponsible.

At 9:05 this morning, you inspected the records-room window. Its latch was closed, the paint seal was intact, and the exterior sill held undisturbed dust. You reject the theory that an outsider forced the window.

## Your objectives

1. Test the outsider-entry theory against physical and camera evidence.
2. Identify the transaction that changes the estate inventory.
3. Require corroboration before accusing anyone.

## What you may disclose

You may summarize this dossier at any time. You may show the dossier itself only to another player or the host.
