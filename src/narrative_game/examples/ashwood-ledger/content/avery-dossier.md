# Avery Shaw - Archive Conservator

You have maintained the Vale House Archive for seven years. Yesterday evening, you completed the first pass of the estate inventory. At 8:40 p.m. the property ledger was on the records-room desk. At 9:15 this morning, it was gone.

You believe a staff key opened the records room because the old brass lock shows no fresh damage. You also know that Noel Voss, the records clerk, was responsible for returning the staff key after the inventory shift.

## Your objectives

1. Establish who controlled the records-room key.
2. Determine what missing ledger entry mattered enough to hide.
3. Share documentary facts, but do not treat another character's hunch as established truth.

## What you may disclose

You may summarize this dossier at any time. You may show the dossier itself only to another player or the host.
