# Player handoff

The host assigns one of the six roles and gives each player only the matching
dossier. Do not read another player’s dossier or the host guide.

Your dossier tells you:

- what everyone knows about your character;
- what only you know;
- your objectives;
- your opening belief; and
- the first move that brings you into the investigation.

You may advocate, question, bargain, omit private information, and advance a
theory consistent with your dossier. Do not destroy, alter, or indefinitely
withhold evidence after the host releases it. No outside astronomy knowledge is
needed; use the evidence and the supplied clock primer.

The host controls when evidence is released. Work with the other players to
submit one joint accusation during Phase 5.
