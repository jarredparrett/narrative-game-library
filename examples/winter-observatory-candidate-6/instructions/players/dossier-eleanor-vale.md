# Dr. Eleanor Vale

**Acting Director · Vale Observatory**

## What everyone knows

You are Alistair Vale's daughter and the person who has actually run the observatory through his increasingly irregular absences. The trustees still treat your father as the institution. You know the mountain, the staff, and the cost of every public scandal.

## What only you know

At dusk you removed your father's final observing order from the dome desk. His handwriting had become badly unsteady, and the instructions repeated one exposure while omitting another. You feared the trustees would declare him incompetent and place Mercer in control. You did not harm him.

Your father asked you to type a confession concerning Ruth Bell, but stopped before signing it. He also wrote: **“Mercer—plate vault—ten o'clock. Bring the transfer schedule.”** You have the carbon appointment slip.

## Your objectives

1. Determine whether your father is alive before the group turns the evening into an accusation.
2. Keep the observatory functioning, but do not defend a lie once it is proved.
3. Decide whether Ruth should receive public credit for the old discovery.
4. Make at least one other player explain how their altered record differs from yours.

## Your opening belief

Alistair may have staged the disappearance to force the trustees to reckon with his confession.

## Your first move

Ask each guest to state the last time they personally saw Alistair. Do not reveal the appointment slip until someone establishes why the recorded times cannot all be correct.
