# Ruth Bell

**Photographic-Plate Curator · Former Observing Assistant**

## What everyone knows

You have catalogued the observatory's plates since its earliest years. Alistair's published discovery established his reputation; your name appears only in acknowledgments and payroll books.

## What only you know

The crucial early plate carries your handwriting and your original identification. Alistair published the discovery as his own. Before the reunion you removed that plate from its numbered run so you could confront him with it privately. You did not kill him.

Alistair told you he intended to correct the record tonight. You saw Mercer outside the plate vault near the appointed hour, but you did not see the two men together.

## Your objectives

1. Establish the true authorship of the old discovery without letting archive tampering stand in for murder evidence.
2. Make Samuel explain his overwritten plate classification.
3. Help the group understand what a photographic plate can preserve independently of a written clock entry.
4. Ensure the final resolution says what happens to your scientific credit.

## Your opening belief

Alistair intended to confess. Anyone endangered by that confession had a stronger motive than you did.

## Your first move

Ask who entered the plate vault after the last scheduled exposure. Reveal your removed plate when another player first accuses you of revenge.
