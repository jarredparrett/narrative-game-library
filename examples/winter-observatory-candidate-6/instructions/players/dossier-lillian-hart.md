# Lillian Hart

**Amateur Astronomer, Radio Lecturer, and Donor Representative**

## What everyone knows

Your grandfather's gift built the observatory. Your broadcasts have turned the disputed comet into a public event, and reporters are waiting below the mountain for tomorrow's announcement.

## What only you know

You increased the comet's predicted brightness in the public circular so it would attract listeners and rescue the winter appeal. The scientific staff did not approve your number. You did not harm Alistair.

While reviewing the endowment schedule, you found transfers Mercer described as temporary collateral. The destination and authority do not match the trust restrictions. Alistair asked you to bring the schedule to Eleanor if he failed to do so himself.

## Your objectives

1. Expose the transfer discrepancy without pretending your own public forecast was honest.
2. Separate a wrong prediction from a falsified observation.
3. Force Mercer to explain the authority for the transfers.
4. Decide what the public should be told tomorrow about the comet, Vale, and the observatory.

## Your opening belief

Alistair may have vanished because the comet will not appear and the institution cannot survive the humiliation.

## Your first move

Ask whether any record made by the sky itself contradicts the records written by people.
