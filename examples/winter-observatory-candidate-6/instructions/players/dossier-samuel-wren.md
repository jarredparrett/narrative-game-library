# Dr. Samuel Wren

**Astronomer · Former Protégé and Professional Rival**

## What everyone knows

You trained under Alistair and now direct a better-funded program of your own. The press describes tonight's reunion as a reconciliation. Neither of you has used that word.

## What only you know

You changed the classification written on a recent plate jacket so the object would support your priority claim. The underlying plate remains untouched, but your handwriting is obvious beneath magnification. You did not harm Alistair.

You prepared a comparison chart for the final plate. The recorded field and telescope position do not agree with the corrected time in the master book. You have not yet determined whether the discrepancy is an error or a falsification.

## Your objectives

1. Demonstrate the sky/clock discrepancy clearly enough that no outside astronomy knowledge is required.
2. Protect your legitimate work while admitting the plate-jacket alteration before someone else exposes it.
3. Test Ruth's revenge theory against physical evidence rather than reputation.
4. End the game with a defensible scientific account of the final sequence.

## Your opening belief

Ruth had the deepest grievance and controlled the archive, but the clock discrepancy does not yet fit that theory.

## Your first move

Ask Rook for the direction and maximum size of the clock drift. Do not call the correction deliberate until the numbers exceed that bound.
