# The Winter Observatory — Host Guide

**Six players · one host · approximately 150 minutes · January 1937**

## The promise

Dr. Alistair Vale disappears during a power failure at his isolated Adirondack observatory. Every guest altered an astronomical record. Five alterations conceal private misconduct; one alteration creates a physically impossible sky and protects a killer.

The solution is fair-play. Players need no outside astronomy knowledge. The records, the clock primer, and the comparison worksheet supply every operation they need.

## Before people arrive

1. Assign Eleanor Vale, Felix Mercer, Ruth Bell, Samuel Wren, Thomas Rook, and Lillian Hart.
2. Give each player only their own dossier.
3. Keep all evidence grouped by the six phases below. Do not give Felix control of released evidence.
4. Place the accusation form aside until Phase 5.
5. Tell the group that a death is central but will not be described graphically, and that there is no supernatural answer.
6. Do not ask players to authenticate handwriting. The entered-by and authorization fields establish documentary attribution; signature shapes remain unauthenticated corroborating marks.

## Canonical truth — host only

Felix Mercer diverted **$18,500.00** from the restricted **$240,000.00** instrument endowment. Alistair discovered the transfer and summoned Mercer to the plate vault at ten o'clock. Mercer killed Vale during the confrontation, moved him through the maintenance route, and concealed him in the disused clock-weight and drive chase beside the solid telescope pier.

Mercer then reversed the sign on the measured sidereal-standard correction. The clock was **56.8 sidereal seconds fast**, so the honest correction is **−56.8 sidereal seconds**. Mercer wrote **+56.8 sidereal seconds**. The two reductions differ by **113.6 sidereal seconds**. Gatehouse Card L-2 converts that interval to **113.3 mean-solar seconds**, placing the false close inside the minute of Mercer's library entry.

## The decisive comparison

Use this only to adjudicate what players have already found.

1. The final plate is `V-1937-0417`.
2. Its field is right ascension `05 37 20.0` with hour angle `+00 10 40.0 W`.
3. The supplied rule is `local sidereal time = right ascension + hour angle`.
4. Therefore the sky requires `05 48 00.0`.
5. The raw S-2 midpoint is `05 48 56.8`.
6. Applying the measured correction `−56.8 seconds` returns `05 48 00.0`.
7. Applying Mercer's written `+56.8 seconds` produces `05 49 53.6`.
8. The false and honest reductions differ by `113.6 sidereal seconds`.
9. Gatehouse chronometer card L-2 ties the honest plate close to `10:02:14.0 P.M. E.S.T.`
10. Card L-2 applies `1 mean-solar second = 1.0027379093508 sidereal seconds`, so the civil displacement is `113.3 mean-solar seconds`.
11. The posted positive sign therefore moves the close to `10:04:07.3 P.M. E.S.T.`, inside Mercer's minute-level library entry at `10:04 P.M. E.S.T.`

The plate is not a magical wall clock. The proof comes from agreement among the identified plate, telescope position, observing log, chronograph contacts, rate notebook, and correction convention.

## Phase 1 — The empty dome (15 minutes)

Read aloud:

> At 10:03 P.M. the main current failed. When the lights returned, Dr. Alistair Vale was absent from the locked observing dome. His winter coat remains in the library. Snow has closed the observatory's private spur, but the public road below the gate is open. The observing records for the last plate do not agree.

Release the storm and power log. Ask each character to state their last contact with Vale and an initial theory. Do not correct false theories yet.

## Phase 2 — Six altered records (35 minutes)

Release the final observing order, early plate envelope, raking-light plate-jacket copy, maintenance log, and public circular. Before Phase 3, require a public round-robin: Eleanor, Ruth, Samuel, Thomas, and Lillian must each identify their own alteration, say why they made it, and name what physical fact it did or did not change. Felix's still-hidden alteration is the sixth record and is resolved in Phase 4.

The intended shift is from “every falsifier may be the killer” to “different alterations have different physical consequences.”

## Phase 3 — The sky as witness (45 minutes)

Release the final plate jacket, plate contact reproduction, observing log, chronograph strip, and the clock primer. Let players compare identifiers before calculating anything.

If they stall, ask:

- Which records describe the same plate?
- Which value comes from the sky and telescope position?
- Which record survived the electrical failure?
- Is Rook's gradual sub-second drift large enough to explain a sign reversal?

## Phase 4 — The false sign (15 minutes)

Release only the correction sheet, radio-rate notebook, RA/HA/LST worksheet, and Gatehouse Chronometer Card L-2. Ask the group to state who measured the clock error, who entered the posted correction, why a sidereal interval must be converted before changing a civil timestamp, and how the two signs map the plate close onto the library minute.

The intended result is bounded: Rook measured a fast standard, Mercer entered the positive sign, and the false close falls within the same recorded civil minute as Mercer's library entry. Do not yet ask for a culprit.

## Phase 5 — The case against the record (20 minutes)

Release the trustee carbon, endowment advice, appointment slip, service-key register, and dome plan. Then release the accusation form.

The group must answer all of the following:

- Who killed Vale?
- What motive is independently documented?
- Which record was altered to create the alibi?
- Which independent records expose that alteration?
- How and where was the disappearance staged?

Accept any of the three complete proof paths below. A timing strand proves falsification; a murder accusation must also connect Mercer to motive, meeting, access, and recovery.

## Three independent proof paths

### Sky, confession, and recovery

Final plate jacket + contact reproduction + observing log + RA/HA/LST worksheet + Gatehouse Card L-2 establish the false sequence and its civil-time consequence. The trustee carbon, appointment slip, and dome plan establish the audit threat, Mercer's meeting, and recovery from chase C.

### Chronograph, money, and access

Chronograph strip + radio-rate notebook + correction sheet + Gatehouse Card L-2 expose Mercer's entered sign and its library-minute consequence. Endowment advice + key register + maintenance log establish financial motive, Mercer's access interval, and the emptied locked chase.

### Authorship, audit, and route

Correction sheet + trustee carbon + endowment advice + appointment slip + key register + dome plan identify the person who entered the false correction, the audit threat, the ten-o'clock confrontation, access interval, and recovery site.

## Recovery interventions

Use the lightest intervention that restores motion.

1. **Drift versus correction:** ask Rook to state the maximum magnitude and direction of his repair error.
2. **Independent witness:** ask what the final plate and telescope position preserve that no handwritten clock entry can change.
3. **Identifier chain:** have the group place every record naming `V-1937-0417` together.
4. **Second route:** if astronomy stalls, direct attention to the transfer reference `T-37-0116-M`, the ten-o'clock appointment, key C, and the drive chase.
5. **Final recovery:** state that a valid accusation may use any one complete proof path, but must still name motive and concealment.

## Resolving the false leads

- **Eleanor** removed the final observing order to conceal her father's tremor and protect governance.
- **Ruth** removed the 1911 plate envelope because it proves Vale appropriated her discovery.
- **Samuel** rewrote a plate-jacket classification to strengthen his priority claim.
- **Thomas** backdated an unauthorized repair that caused less than one second of drift.
- **Lillian** changed the predicted magnitude from 8.1 to 5.8 to protect publicity and fundraising.
- **Felix** alone changed a measured correction in a way that makes the independent astronomical record impossible.

## Phase 6 — What the record remembers (20 minutes)

Confirm the body was recovered from the drive chase without graphic description. Restore Ruth Bell's scientific credit. Ask each player what should be disclosed publicly about Vale, the financial diversion, the comet forecast, and the observatory.

End on the theme: **a record can be revised, but reality leaves independent witnesses.**
